# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ChangeSafe community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting
constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include sexualized language or imagery,
trolling or insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing
others' private information without permission, and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel when available, or by opening a minimal non-sensitive issue
asking the maintainers for a private contact path. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
